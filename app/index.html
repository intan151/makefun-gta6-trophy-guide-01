    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough — there is no mission replay, but free-roam continues after the story and everything needed for 100% stays reachable. PowerPyx puts the whole run at about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, with A Long Road (100% completion) the longest grind. Work the phases in order; the platinum pops with the last trophy you earn.

    Do first, in Chapter 1: meditate at Jason’s house — the one meditation spot PowerPyx flags as possibly missable. See Missables.

    Cheats off: using cheats disables trophies and saving, so keep them off for the whole run (GTABase / TechWiser, via EGamers).

    100% is lenient: the checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, visible after the story) needs only 70 of 80 challenges — roughly 60% of true completion, per PowerPyx.

      Missable trophies

      Story trophies — not missable

      These 17 unlock by playing the story: chapter completions, named missions and character finales. There is no mission replay, but none of them can be failed permanently — finish the mission and the trophy pops.
